## A03: Testing a Directional Population Correlation

### T04-A03-V01: Practice time and reasoning score

Before seeing the values, the directional alternative was specified as $H_1:\rho>0$. (a) Calculate Pearson's $r=s_{xy}/(s_xs_y)$ and state $H_0$.
